SimCheck Client Data Policy

SimCheck, operated by Tellbloom, Inc. · Effective October 27, 2026

In short
  • Your content is never used to train AI models, either by us or by the AI providers we use.
  • Your content is never reused in work for anyone else, and never sold, published or used in marketing.
  • We delete our copies within 30 days of delivering the work, or sooner if you ask.
  • Only the providers named on this page receive your content, and only to do the work.

1. What this policy covers

SimCheck is a service that tests and reviews AI roleplay training simulators for business customers (each a "Customer"). It is operated by Tellbloom, Inc. ("we"). This policy describes how we handle Client Content: simulator configuration and prompts, test conversation transcripts, evaluations and scores, documents a Customer gives us for the work, and the reports we produce from them. It includes any material in that content that came from the Customer's own clients.

This policy adds to any confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement between Tellbloom and a Customer. Where the two differ, the agreement governs.

This policy covers SimCheck only. Tellbloom’s other products have their own terms, and this policy does not describe them.

2. How Client Content is processed

  • The Customer's simulator platform. We run test conversations inside the Customer's own simulator platform (for example, Cicero), using access the Customer grants. Content held there remains under the Customer's own agreement with that platform.
  • Our computer. SimCheck runs on a computer controlled by Tellbloom. Client Content is stored there in a folder that is kept outside any cloud-synced folder and excluded from source control. It is never committed to a code repository.
  • AI providers. SimCheck sends Client Content to the AI providers below to play the trainee in test conversations, review transcripts against the Customer's standards, and edit report wording. Model training on our content is turned off on both of our accounts. Each request starts a new, standalone session with no saved conversation history and no memory features, so content from one job is not available in another.

4. What we never do

  • Use Client Content to train or fine-tune any AI model, or let a provider do so.
  • Use one Customer's content in work for another Customer, or build shared knowledge from it.
  • Sell Client Content, or use it for advertising.
  • Publish it, or show it in marketing, case studies or demonstrations without the Customer's written permission.
  • Share it with anyone beyond the providers listed above and the people working on the engagement.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Our copies of Client Content are deleted once they have gone 30 days without use. That means no later than 30 days after we deliver the work they supported.
  • The Customer can ask us to delete its content sooner, at any time.
  • The Customer's QA standards and checklists, which every test run needs, are kept for the length of the engagement and deleted when it ends.
  • We keep a minimal run record with no content (identifiers, timestamps and trial counts) to enforce agreed run limits and for billing.
  • Delivered reports belong to the Customer. Our copy is deleted under the same 30-day rule.
  • Test conversations held in the Customer's simulator platform stay in the Customer's account, under the Customer's control.
  • Copies held by AI providers are deleted on each provider's own schedule.

6. Access and security

  • Only the people working on the engagement can access Client Content.
  • Passwords and access credentials are never stored in source control.
  • If a security incident affects Client Content, we will notify the Customer without undue delay.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and current Customers will be told before the change applies to their content.
